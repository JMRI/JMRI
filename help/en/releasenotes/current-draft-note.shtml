Hardware Support

  • XpressNet, Roco Z21 and DCC4PC: a "busy" answer during a burst of commands no longer leaves messages and their replies out of step until JMRI is restarted.
  • A serial connection that recovers from a failure (such as a CANUSB4 or other GridConnect adapter, or a LocoBuffer) is now set up again when the port reopens at the first attempt; before, it showed as connected but sent and received nothing. An error opening the port at the first attempt no longer stops the retries.
  • Connections (NCE, C/MRI, CAN, XpressNet and others): after a connection recovers from a failure, messages are no longer sent out of turn or their replies given to the wrong listener.

Dispatcher

  • Dispatcher's Options now offer the CUSTOM layout scale, with its ratio in a box beside the scale list, for a layout whose scale isn't in the list. There is one CUSTOM scale, shared with the Timetable.
  • Tools > Train Tracking > Set-up Checks now says when Dispatcher's Layout Scale, in its Options, is not the Layout Scale in Preferences > Warrants. Speed profiles give scale speeds at the Warrants scale, while Dispatcher works out scale lengths and speeds at its own.
  • With automatic allocation and the new option Prevent Deadlock, off unless checked, Active Trains can no longer end up waiting for each other for good, such as two trains each holding half of a single line. A train is then given track from one place it can stop to the next, all at once, and only where every Active Train can still run its Transit. A train waiting for track gains a step of priority for each minute it waits. Trains started where they can't all run their Transits are named in a warning. Unchecked, Sections are allocated as before.
  • With automatic allocation, an automatic train is no longer held for good by a meet planned after it had already been given the road beyond its meeting place. Two trains running the same way round a circuit could be taken for trains coming towards each other; one was then told to wait in a station for the other, which needed track the waiting train already held. Dispatcher now drops such a meet, as it does when a train is already past the meeting place.

Miscellaneous

  • Tools > Train Tracking > Measurements lists, for each Block and way through it, how many times trains have crossed it this session, their average speed, how that compares with their lead locos' speed profiles, and how often its detector dropped out under a train. A Block whose occupancy sensor is an internal sensor is followed but not measured, except with simulated trains. Nothing uses the measurements yet.
  • The first-time start-up wizard finds its list of languages into a list of its own, so building that page twice at once can no longer break the sort with "Comparison method violates its general contract".
  • In a profile whose connections are all simulators, a loco marker dragged onto a Block's track on a Layout Editor panel, in operating mode, places a simulated train there: a window asks for its length, how it is detected, the way it faces and where its front is, filled in so that OK is usually enough, and offers to tell train tracking the train is there as well. The Block's menu takes the train off again. With any connection to a layout, a dropped marker does what it did before. See the Simulated Trains page.
